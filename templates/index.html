What is a carbon footprint?

A carbon footprint is the amount of greenhouse gases, mainly carbon dioxide (CO₂), generated by the activities of a person, household, organization, product or service. EcoTrack turns everyday activity into a simple estimate you can learn from.

Why track it?

Awareness becomes useful when you can act on it.

A footprint estimate is not a score to feel guilty about. It is a starting point: a way to see patterns, choose one practical change, and notice what happens over time.

Learn the language

Carbon emissions, simply explained.

CO₂

Carbon dioxide

A greenhouse gas released by processes such as burning fuel and changing land use.

CO₂e

Carbon dioxide equivalent

A shared unit that expresses the warming effect of different greenhouse gases in one number.

↗

Direct and indirect

Some emissions come directly from an activity; others are linked to electricity, products and services.

About EcoTrack

Environmental science, made personal.

EcoTrack was created to make carbon-footprint awareness understandable and accessible. It brings travel, food, electricity and waste into one calm workspace, then gives you a practical next step instead of an overwhelming list.

All results are estimates based on documented factors and the information you provide. Real emissions vary by route, technology, supplier and behavior.
